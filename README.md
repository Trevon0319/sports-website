  <section>
    <h2>⚽ Football (Soccer)</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>What is it?</strong> A team sport played between two teams of 11 players. The goal is to score by getting the ball into the opponent's net.</li>
      <li><strong>Major Events:</strong> FIFA World Cup, UEFA Champions League, English Premier League</li>
      <li><strong>Famous Players:</strong> Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Pelé, Neymar</li>
      <li><strong>Fun Fact:</strong> Football is the most popular sport in the world, with over 4 billion fans.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>🏀 Basketball</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>What is it?</strong> A fast-paced game with two teams of five players. Teams score by shooting a ball through a hoop 10 feet above the ground.</li>
      <li><strong>Major Events:</strong> NBA, FIBA World Cup, NCAA March Madness</li>
      <li><strong>Famous Players:</strong> Michael Jordan, LeBron James, Stephen Curry, Kobe Bryant</li>
      <li><strong>Fun Fact:</strong> The first basketball game was played with a soccer ball and peach baskets!</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>🏈 American Football</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>What is it?</strong> Played by two teams of 11. The objective is to carry or throw the ball into the end zone.</li>
      <li><strong>Major Events:</strong> NFL Super Bowl, College Football Playoff, Pro Bowl</li>
      <li><strong>Famous Players:</strong> Tom Brady, Peyton Manning, Jerry Rice</li>
      <li><strong>Fun Fact:</strong> The Super Bowl is one of the most-watched events in the U.S., with over 100 million viewers.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>⚾ Baseball</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>What is it?</strong> Two teams take turns batting and fielding. Players score by hitting a ball and running around four bases.</li>
      <li><strong>Major Events:</strong> MLB World Series, Japan Series, World Baseball Classic</li>
      <li><strong>Famous Players:</strong> Babe Ruth, Jackie Robinson, Shohei Ohtani</li>
      <li><strong>Fun Fact:</strong> A perfect game in baseball is when no opposing player reaches base — very rare!</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>🎾 Tennis</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>What is it?</strong> A racket sport played one-on-one or in doubles. Players hit the ball over a net, trying to score points.</li>
      <li><strong>Major Events (Grand Slams):</strong> Wimbledon, US Open, French Open, Australian Open</li>
      <li><strong>Famous Players:</strong> Serena Williams, Roger Federer, Novak Djokovic, Rafael Nadal</li>
      <li><strong>Fun Fact:</strong> The longest tennis match ever lasted over 11 hours (Wimbledon 2010).</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>🏏 Cricket</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>What is it?</strong> A bat-and-ball game with 11 players per team. Runs are scored by hitting the ball and running between wickets.</li>
      <li><strong>Major Events:</strong> ICC Cricket World Cup, The Ashes, Indian Premier League (IPL)</li>
      <li><strong>Famous Players:</strong> Sachin Tendulkar, Virat Kohli, MS Dhoni, Ben Stokes</li>
      <li><strong>Fun Fact:</strong> A cricket match can last from 3 hours to 5 days!</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>🥊 Boxing</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>What is it?</strong> Two competitors fight using their fists under strict rules, wearing gloves.</li>
      <li><strong>Major Events:</strong> WBC, WBA, IBF World Championships, Olympic Boxing</li>
      <li><strong>Famous Boxers:</strong> Muhammad Ali, Mike Tyson, Floyd Mayweather Jr.</li>
      <li><strong>Fun Fact:</strong> Ali's fight "The Rumble in the Jungle" drew a global audience of over 1 billion!</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>⛳ Golf</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>What is it?</strong> Players use clubs to hit balls into a series of holes with as few strokes as possible.</li>
      <li><strong>Major Events (Majors):</strong> The Masters, The Open Championship, PGA Championship, U.S. Open</li>
      <li><strong>Famous Golfers:</strong> Tiger Woods, Jack Nicklaus, Rory McIlroy</li>
      <li><strong>Fun Fact:</strong> The longest recorded golf drive is over 500 yards!</li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section>
    <h2>🏒 Ice Hockey</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>What is it?</strong> Played on ice between two teams of six. Players use sticks to hit a puck into the opponent’s net.</li>
      <li><strong>Major Events:</strong> NHL Stanley Cup, Winter Olympics, IIHF World Championships</li>
      <li><strong>Famous Players:</strong> Wayne Gretzky, Sidney Crosby, Alexander Ovechkin</li>
      <li><strong>Fun Fact:</strong> Pucks are frozen before games so they don’t bounce.</li>
    </ul>
  </section>
