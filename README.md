# 🏆 Major Sports of the World

## ⚽ Football (Soccer)
- **What is it?** A team sport played between two teams of 11 players. The goal is to score by getting the ball into the opponent's net.
- **Major Events:** FIFA World Cup, UEFA Champions League, English Premier League
- **Famous Players:** Lionel Messi, Cristiano Ronaldo, Pelé, Neymar
- **Fun Fact:** Football is the most popular sport in the world, with over 4 billion fans.

## 🏀 Basketball
- **What is it?** A fast-paced game with two teams of five players. Teams score by shooting a ball through a hoop 10 feet above the ground.
- **Major Events:** NBA, FIBA World Cup, NCAA March Madness
- **Famous Players:** Michael Jordan, LeBron James, Stephen Curry, Kobe Bryant
- **Fun Fact:** The first basketball game was played with a soccer ball and peach baskets!

## 🏈 American Football
- **What is it?** Played by two teams of 11. The objective is to carry or throw the ball into the end zone.
- **Major Events:** NFL Super Bowl, College Football Playoff, Pro Bowl
- **Famous Players:** Tom Brady, Peyton Manning, Jerry Rice
- **Fun Fact:** The Super Bowl is one of the most-watched events in the U.S., with over 100 million viewers.

## ⚾ Baseball
- **What is it?** Two teams take turns batting and fielding. Players score by hitting a ball and running around four bases.
- **Major Events:** MLB World Series, Japan Series, World Baseball Classic
- **Famous Players:** Babe Ruth, Jackie Robinson, Shohei Ohtani
- **Fun Fact:** A perfect game in baseball is when no opposing player reaches base — very rare!

## 🎾 Tennis
- **What is it?** A racket sport played one-on-one or in doubles. Players hit the ball over a net, trying to score points.
- **Major Events (Grand Slams):** Wimbledon, US Open, French Open, Australian Open
- **Famous Players:** Serena Williams, Roger Federer, Novak Djokovic, Rafael Nadal
- **Fun Fact:** The longest tennis match ever lasted over 11 hours (Wimbledon 2010).

## 🏏 Cricket
- **What is it?** A bat-and-ball game with 11 players per team. Runs are scored by hitting the ball and running between wickets.
- **Major Events:** ICC Cricket World Cup, The Ashes, Indian Premier League (IPL)
- **Famous Players:** Sachin Tendulkar, Virat Kohli, MS Dhoni, Ben Stokes
- **Fun Fact:** A cricket match can last from 3 hours to 5 days!

## 🥊 Boxing
- **What is it?** Two competitors fight using their fists under strict rules, wearing gloves.
- **Major Events:** WBC, WBA, IBF World Championships, Olympic Boxing
- **Famous Boxers:** Muhammad Ali, Mike Tyson, Floyd Mayweather Jr.
- **Fun Fact:** Ali's fight "The Rumble in the Jungle" drew a global audience of over 1 billion!

## ⛳ Golf
- **What is it?** Players use clubs to hit balls into a series of holes with as few strokes as possible.
- **Major Events (Majors):** The Masters, The Open Championship, PGA Championship, U.S. Open
- **Famous Golfers:** Tiger Woods, Jack Nicklaus, Rory McIlroy
- **Fun Fact:** The longest recorded golf drive is over 500 yards!

## 🏒 Ice Hockey
- **What is it?** Played on ice between two teams of six. Players use sticks to hit a puck into the opponent’s net.
- **Major Events:** NHL Stanley Cup, Winter Olympics, IIHF World Championships
- **Famous Players:** Wayne Gretzky, Sidney Crosby, Alexander Ovechkin
- **Fun Fact:** Pucks are frozen before games so they don’t bounce.

## 🏃 Athletics (Track & Field)
- **What is it?** Events including running, jumping, and throwing.
- **Major Events:** Summer Olympics, World Athletics Championships
- **Famous Athletes:** Usain Bolt, Carl Lewis, Allyson Felix
- **Fun Fact:** Usain Bolt's top speed was 27.8 mph — faster than most cars in city traffic.

## 🏓 Table Tennis
- **What is it?** A fast-paced sport where players hit a lightweight ball across a small table.
- **Major Events:** World Table Tennis Championships, Olympic Games
- **Famous Players:** Ma Long, Deng Yaping, Zhang Jike
- **Fun Fact:** A ping pong ball can travel at over 70 mph!
